Snap Dust

A local dust-disappearance camera effect.

  1. Enable for the meeting site. This reloads the tab; do it before joining.
  2. Start your camera. Step out, then select Save empty room in the meeting's floating panel.
  3. Return and click Disappear, press Alt + Shift + D, or enable snap listening.
  4. Click Reappear to return.

Browser meetings only, not desktop apps. Keep the room and camera still. Snap detection is experimental; claps and clicks may trigger it. This is not a privacy or background-redaction tool.
